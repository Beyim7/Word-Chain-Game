How to play

You are given a starting word. Type the next word in the chain.

Every two neighbors must form a real phrase or compound.

BIRTHDAY → PARTY → TIME → MACHINE → GUN

  • time machine

Solve in order. The first letter of each hidden word is shown. Just type on your keyboard: the word itself is the input. Hints reveal one extra letter and cost coins.

All 50 levels and your progress stay on this device. No internet required.
